⏰ Chase & collect payments

Stay on top of receivables without the awkward.

Screenshot of Chase & collect payments — open it in the app →

How to use it

  1. From Collections, sort by most overdue.
  2. Send an approved reminder, or log a promise-to-pay with a date.
  3. Escalate a stubborn balance to a person for a call.
  4. Watch the outstanding and collected totals move as you go.

Tips

Tip. Set up an automation rule to remind you when an invoice passes 15 days (see “Automation rules”).
