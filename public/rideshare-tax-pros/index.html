IRS Gig Economy Tax Center ↗
What if I drive for both Uber and Lyft?+

The service is designed around rideshare and gig work, including income from multiple platforms. Share the full picture with your tax professional so the engagement reflects your work.

Is this useful for my first year driving?+

Yes. The process starts with the support you need and the records requested for your situation. You can ask questions before agreeing to a service.

How much does tax preparation cost?+

Pricing depends on the tax year, forms, complexity and services involved. A fee schedule is not yet published here. Confirm the scope and fee before beginning an engagement.

Do you guarantee a refund or a particular saving?+

No. Your outcome depends on your circumstances, records and applicable tax rules. Financial figures shown in the artwork are illustrative and are not a quote, calculation or promise of your result.

Can I sign in or start filing here today?+

Client onboarding and private workspace access are opening soon. The public website currently explains services; it does not accept returns, tax-document uploads or payments.

What is the Shuug client workspace?+

Shuug is the business platform behind the firm’s Tax Firm workflow. The branded workspace will organize client work and staff follow-up. Any external filing service is separate, and sign-in details will be provided when access opens.

How should I send sensitive tax documents?+

Wait for verified instructions from your tax professional and use their approved private document channel. This public website has no tax-document upload form. Keep taxpayer IDs, bank details and tax documents out of general contact messages.
