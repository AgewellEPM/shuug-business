Accessibility.

Last updated November 2, 2026

We aim to make Gig Tax Pros understandable and usable across devices and ways of interacting. Accessibility is ongoing work; this statement is not a claim of independent certification.

Features included

  • A readable HTML layout for smaller screens, with text that reflows and no disabled browser zoom.
  • Keyboard access to navigation and service controls, with visible focus indicators and a skip-to-content link.
  • Native expandable service and FAQ sections that work without a mouse.
  • Labeled dialog controls and native dialog behavior, including Escape to close.
  • Reduced-motion support and generous touch areas for navigation and footer links.
  • Text headings, service descriptions and control labels alongside the supplied desktop artwork.

If something is difficult to use

Please share the page, device and the difficulty you encountered through the contact method published in our contact section. Direct contact details are being finalized. Do not include sensitive taxpayer information in accessibility feedback.

Continuing review

Visual review on real devices and feedback from people using assistive technology remain part of improving this website. Linked external websites and future private workspaces may provide their own accessibility information.
