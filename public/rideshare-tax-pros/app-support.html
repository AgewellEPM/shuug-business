GIG TAX PROS APP

App support.

For help with Gig Tax Pros, email support@gigtax.pro. Include the app version shown in Account, your device model and operating-system version, the steps you tried and what happened. Do not send passwords, taxpayer IDs, bank details, full tax documents or unredacted screenshots of private records.

Your records

Records are saved locally before any optional account sync. If you see a storage error, preserve the installation and its data while requesting help. Export important records before deleting or reinstalling the app. Signing out does not delete an account's local journal.

Mileage and reminders

Check location and motion permissions in device Settings and the driving-assistance controls in Account. Start or finish a drive only when safe. GPS gaps and force-quitting can cause missing mileage; review saved trips before relying on their totals.

Uber, Lyft and other offers

While parked, choose a screenshot or enter the offer details. Review extracted pay, total distance, time and costs before saving a comparison. Optional screen sharing must be started through Apple's control on a physical iPhone. Gig Tax Pros cannot read other apps' private notification inboxes and does not accept or decline jobs for you.

Accounts and connections

Firm accounts require an invitation and a configured online workspace. A connection marked pending or unavailable has not completed. Bank and gig-provider connections depend on actual service availability and authorization. Contact your firm if you cannot activate an invitation or identify its workspace.

Privacy and deletion requests

Read the app privacy policy. Send privacy, access or deletion requests to support@gigtax.pro; we may need to verify your identity and coordinate legally required retention with your firm. Do not send sensitive tax documents in the request.

Tax questions

Gig Tax Pros organizes records and provides estimates; it does not automatically file returns or guarantee earnings or tax outcomes. Discuss individualized tax questions with your tax professional.
