GIG TAX PROS APP

App privacy policy.

Last updated November 17, 2026

Gig Tax Pros provides tools for organizing driving records, expenses, receipts and gig-offer comparisons. This policy describes the Gig Tax Pros iPhone, iPad and Android apps. For the public website, see the website privacy notice. Contact support@gigtax.pro for app support or privacy questions.

Your local journal and permissions

You may use the local journal without connecting a client account. You choose which trips, income, expenses, notes, photos and scans to save. Receipt and offer text recognition runs on your device. Location permission supports mileage recording and requested maps or office searches. Optional driving and stop assistance also uses location in the background and motion activity. Notification permission supports reminders and offer alerts. Camera permission is requested for receipt scanning; choosing a photo uses the system picker.

Turn assistance off in Account and end any active drive to stop recording. GPS gaps, force-quitting and permission changes may leave incomplete mileage. Recorded route files are kept in private app storage and excluded from ordinary mileage-record sync. Optional encrypted journal archives can include location-bearing tracker and planning state, as described below. Apple Maps receives requested search terms, map areas and route endpoints. Your device and backup settings control system backups.

Offer screenshots and optional screen sharing

You choose an image or explicitly start the platform screen-sharing control (Apple ReplayKit on iPhone/iPad or Android MediaProjection). Gig Tax Pros reads visible offer text on-device. It does not retain or upload full screen images, video or transcripts. Parsed offer fields are held locally for review and can be included in an optional encrypted journal archive. A source image in your Photos library remains there unless you delete it. The app retains a bounded recent observation history for diagnostics and any offer comparisons you explicitly save.

The operating system shows a recording or screen-sharing indicator. Stop through Apple's recording control or Android's screen-sharing indicator or Stop reading notification; reader permission expires after four hours or when you disable reading or change accounts. The app does not access another app's private notification inbox or automatically accept or decline jobs. Stop sharing before opening sensitive unrelated screens.

Android notification access and SDKs

On Android, you can separately grant notification access in system Settings and choose the gig apps whose offer notifications may be read during an enabled reader shift. The app filters notifications to the apps you select; it does not control those apps or accept jobs. Full notification transcripts are not retained or uploaded. Parsed offer fields are kept in the local journal and can be included in an optional encrypted archive. Turn off the reader in the app or revoke notification access in Android Settings to stop this feature.

Android mileage recording uses a foreground service with a persistent notification after you start a drive. End the drive to stop that recording. Android device backup and transfer are disabled for this app, so uninstalling or losing a phone can erase phone-only records and device keys. Export important records and verify any configured recovery arrangement first.

The Android app uses Google ML Kit for receipt and offer text recognition. ML Kit processes the input images and recognized text on-device; it sends device and app information, installation identifiers, and API performance or usage metrics to Google for diagnostics and analytics. It may contact Google for model or compatibility updates. These metrics differ from the receipt images or recognized financial text. Read Google's ML Kit disclosure and Google's privacy policy.

When configured and used, the Android Maps SDK can send device and request metadata, diagnostic information, map interactions and a Maps identifier to Google. Requested route comparisons send chosen endpoints through the firm's authenticated routing service. Opening external directions uses your selected maps app. Office maps may request OpenStreetMap tiles, which expose technical request information such as the device's IP address. Review Google's Maps SDK disclosure. These services are used for maps, diagnostics and service improvement; the app has no advertising SDK or advertising-tracking purpose.

Account modes and your choices

The local journal works without cloud sync. Published mobile builds do not yet include the new client-only workspace update; using their local journal does not by itself upload it to that workspace. The new hosted portal uses a private account code or a connected sign-in method, account and authenticator checks, and the client’s original encryption key. New mobile updates remain separate from the currently published builds; check the version and connection status shown in your app.

Earlier firm-connected account modes may use an email, display name, account identifier and platform-protected session credential. Where that older sharing is configured and chosen, supported trips, income, expenses, notes, selected receipts, chat and entered ratings can be shared with authorized firm staff. Those older modes are separate from the new client-only profile. Accounts keep separate local journals, and recorded historical route files are excluded from ordinary record sync. Do not treat pending setup or an unacknowledged upload as successful sharing.

Google and Apple sign-in

Where enabled, Continue with Google or Continue with Apple opens the provider’s own sign-in page. We verify its signed identity and use a protected representation of its stable account identifier to connect the sign-in method to your existing account. We do not automatically match or merge accounts by email. This flow requests identity authentication, without access to Google mail, Drive, contacts, photos or payment accounts. The provider receives the sign-in request, app identity and ordinary technical information under its own privacy practices.

Account and authenticator checks still apply. Gig Tax Pros does not send your tax records, receipt images, client encryption key or backup passphrase to a sign-in provider through this flow. Apple authorization credentials needed to manage and revoke the connection are encrypted in the private server’s credential storage. Disconnect a sign-in method in account settings; remaining app sessions can be ended separately. You can also review the app’s permissions in your Google or Apple account. See Google’s privacy policy and Apple’s privacy policy.

Optional client-only workspace sync

The new workspace encrypts financial record contents for the client’s key before upload. You hold the decryption key and control the encrypted backup used to recover that same key. This profile has no administrator or firm recovery recipient, and the administrator’s ordinary workspace access cannot open these new encrypted contents. A valid sign-in does not replace possession of your encryption key.

You choose whether to connect a compatible app and enable encrypted sync. Once supported setup is complete and you import the original encrypted key backup locally, that app can upload saved Journal changes and receipt files automatically while it is running, online and unlocked, and retry saved encrypted changes when reopened. Client-only mobile updates have not yet been released through the app stores. The hosted portal can open supported encrypted records after sign-in and local key unlock; do not assume every published app can sync, download or restore them.

Journal snapshots can include saved trips, income, expenses, notes, chat, entered ratings, preferences, offers, planning destinations and location-bearing tracker state. Receipt bytes are separate encrypted objects. Historical route files stay on the phone. Encryption does not make account use anonymous: the server retains ciphertext, public account/key bindings, opaque account and object identifiers, revisions, deletion markers, authentication information and operational metadata. Google and Apple provider connections, sign-in checks and technical request logs are separate from financial-content encryption.

Keep the encrypted key backup and its separate passphrase somewhere you control. If every usable client key is lost and you cannot unlock a client-held backup, the encrypted contents are unrecoverable. Password resets, authenticator recovery codes and Google or Apple sign-in do not recreate the encryption key. Support staff cannot supply a replacement key that opens those contents.

Earlier shared records and encrypted archives

Earlier account or archive formats may have shared records directly with authorized firm staff or encrypted copies for both a client and a separately held firm recovery key. Those original recipients can still open their copies. The new client-only profile does not revoke, erase or automatically migrate older shared records, recovery copies, exports or backups.

An older journal archive can include precise or approximate location from tracker state, saved planning destinations or work areas, as well as other saved Journal content. Historical route files and receipt image bytes are separate. Availability and approvals depend on the specific older version and workspace. Review an explicit migration or export before changing accounts; keep the originals until recovery has been verified.

Bank and other service connections

Statement files remain in the local inbox until you review and save entries. Optional direct bank linking uses Plaid and the bank's sign-in. Gig Tax Pros does not receive your bank password. A configured firm stores encrypted provider access tokens and downloaded transactions, and the app retrieves supported feed records for review. Disconnecting stops future access; previously imported history remains subject to retention and deletion choices.

Bank, gig-account and tax-workspace services require actual provider configuration and your authorization. Opening a provider app or reading a screenshot does not connect that account. Apple services and authorized providers process data under their own policies. Review Plaid's privacy policy when linking a bank. Office booking and external links open the selected provider; confirm what you submit there.

Tracking and use of information

The app has no advertising SDK and does not use your data for advertising tracking. Account identifiers, financial records and selected content are used to provide the requested account, journal and workspace functions. Network services may process technical request information needed to operate and secure their services. Do not include passwords or unnecessary taxpayer identifiers in notes, chat or support email.

Retention, export and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them or remove the app, subject to your device backup settings. Signing out hides an account's journal but does not delete it. Export important records before removing the app. Entry deletions sync as deleted records so an old copy is not automatically restored; historical firm records, audit logs and backups may remain.

To request account or shared-data deletion, or ask about access and retention, email support@gigtax.pro and identify the connected firm without including sensitive records. We may verify your identity before acting. Your tax firm may need to retain records for legal, tax or engagement requirements; request details from that firm. Disconnecting a bank does not itself delete previously imported financial records.

Security and your choices

The app uses private app storage, platform data protection, Keychain and HTTPS connections. These protections do not guarantee against every risk. Do not share invitation links, passwords, client keys, backup files or backup passphrases. You control location, motion, camera and notification permissions in device Settings. Keep account and device recovery arrangements current.

Questions and policy changes

Email support@gigtax.pro for questions or privacy requests. Avoid sending taxpayer IDs, bank details, identity documents or full tax returns by general email. This policy will be updated when information-handling features change; the date above identifies this version.
