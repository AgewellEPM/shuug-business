More than software — it works for you

AI Handlers do the busywork

Say what you wish you didn't have to do anymore. Shuug builds a Handler from your real data — customers, orders, calendar, staff — and it works Off, on Approval, or fully On. You're always in control.

Answer the phone & book Handler

An AI receptionist books, reschedules and confirms appointments while you work.

Chase what's owed Handler

Sends invoice reminders on the right day and answers "what do I owe" for customers.

Never run out Handler

Watches stock and ingredients and drafts the reorder before you sell out.

Build the schedule Handler

Drafts next week's staff rota from your hours and warns about overtime first.

Map my week Roadmap

Talk through your notes and Shuug turns them into an actionable plan of what to do next.

Answer customers Handler

Handles order status, hours and FAQs across email, SMS and chat — you approve what it can say.

Handlers are approval-gated by default and never act outside what you allow. Live phone/SMS/email needs a connected channel in Integrations.
