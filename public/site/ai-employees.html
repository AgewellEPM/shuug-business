Always your call

Off, Approval, or On

Every AI employee has one dial. Move it when you trust it — never before.

⏸️

Off

It drafts and suggests, but does nothing on its own. Watch it work before you trust it.

✋

Approval

It prepares each action and waits for your yes. One tap to send, book or post.

▶️

On

It runs the routine work itself, within the limits you set — and logs everything.
