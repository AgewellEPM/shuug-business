One dashboard, not a pile of apps

Your entire business, funneled into one place.

Most companies run on a stack of apps and a shoebox of receipts, then pay a bookkeeper to stitch it together. Shuug is one platform designed to funnel every part of your company into a single dashboard: operations, bookkeeping, AI employees, workflows and business intelligence share the same records — so nothing is entered twice, nothing is reconciled by hand, and you stop jumping tool to tool. 1000% free, 100% customizable, built around you.

What's inside

The full suite, one dashboard

Not features bolted onto a sidebar — one platform where operations, the books, your AI employees and your BI are the same records. 1000% free and 100% customizable.

🏃

Business operations

Orders, menus & recipes, inventory, reorder points, bookings, jobs and work orders — the daily work of your company, built for your trade and priced into COGS and the books.

📚

Real double-entry bookkeeping

A true general ledger with journal, trial balance, P&L, Balance Sheet and sales tax. Sales, payments and expenses post themselves; the statements are derived from the ledger, so they always balance.

🤖

AI employees

Tell Shuug what you wish you didn't have to do; it hires an AI employee — a Handler — built from your real data. It answers the phone, chases invoices, restocks and builds the schedule. Off, Approval or On — you're the boss.

⚡

Workflows & automation

Rules that move work for you: when this happens, do that. Route approvals, trigger reminders, update records — no code, no separate automation app open.

📊

Business intelligence

Every number from every corner in one live dashboard — revenue, cash, stock, jobs, staff and margin. Know how the whole company is doing right now, without exporting a spreadsheet.

💳

Invoicing & getting paid

Quotes turn into invoices, invoices into payments — with aging, reminders, promises to pay, partial payments, fees and refunds. Collections and a 12-week cash-flow forecast built in.

🕒

Staff & payroll

Time clock and timesheets, scheduling and shifts, overtime and hours-by-job that roll straight into payroll and your labor cost.

🧩

Build your own tools

A module SDK and no-code trackers. Add the exact tool your business needs, reuse it freely, or hand it to a dev to build on top of Shuug — it's designed to be extended.

🔌

Plug into everything · 300+ apps

Direct hookups for Shopify, Amazon, Stripe, PayPal, Square, Slack, GoDaddy, QuickBooks and banks — plus 300+ more through Zapier. Bring your whole team and every tool into one place; import from QuickBooks or migrate hard legacy data in with verified evidence.

🏷️

White-label & yours

Your name, colors and logo, applied app-wide. Own the system, host it, move it, export it, reuse it. The software is 1000% free — you're never trapped and never up-sold.
