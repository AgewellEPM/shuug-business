Everything a CRM & customers needs — in one place

Questions

Free, real, and yours

Straight answers about running a CRM & customers on a free, open platform.

Is Shuug really free for crm & customerss?
Yes — the software is 1000% free and open source (MIT-licensed). There are no per-seat fees and no locked "pro" tier; you only pay for hosting and any help importing old data. You can even self-host it yourself for free.
Do I need accounting knowledge to run the books?
No. Every sale, client / parent portal and expense posts to real double-entry books automatically, so your P&L, balance sheet and sales tax are always right. You run the crm & customers; the bookkeeping keeps itself.
Can it replace my current crm & client records tool?
Yes — CRM & client records is built in and wired to everything else, so there's nothing to sync. It also connects to Shopify, Amazon, Stripe, QuickBooks and 300+ apps if you'd rather keep a tool you already love.
What happens when I grow or add locations?
Shuug scales from a solo owner to multi-site and enterprise without switching systems — add staff, roles, locations and your own modules. Because it's open source you can white-label and extend it however you need, and your data is always yours to export.
