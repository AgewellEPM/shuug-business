Your AI employees

AI employees that run the busywork

You don't build agents or prompts. Tell Shuug what you wish you didn't have to do anymore, and it hires an AI employee — a Handler — built from your real data: customers, orders, calendar, staff. Off, on Approval, or fully On. You're always the boss.

Answer the phone & book Handler

An AI receptionist books, reschedules and confirms appointments while you work.

Chase what's owed Handler

Sends invoice reminders on the right day and answers "what do I owe" for customers.

Never run out Handler

Watches stock & ingredients and drafts the reorder before you sell out.

Build the schedule Handler

Drafts next week's staff rota from real hours and warns about overtime first.

Map my week Roadmap

Talk through your notes; Shuug turns them into an actionable plan of what to do next — your own monday-style workspace.

Answer customers Handler

Order status, hours and FAQs across email, SMS & chat — you approve what it can say.
