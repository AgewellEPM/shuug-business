The engine, not a dashboard

One door, not 40 SaaS APIs

A person, your website and an AI handler all reach the business through the same permissioned operations. Nobody flips a status by hand — every change passes the rules, commits atomically, and leaves an audit trail.

  • ✦Guarded — approval needs evidence, scheduling needs a free bay
  • ✦Atomic — state, history & audit commit together or not at all
  • ✦Idempotent — a retried request never charges twice
  • ✦Audited — who asked, what was checked, what changed, when
