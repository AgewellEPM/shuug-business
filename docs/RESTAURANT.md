# Restaurant operations

Choose **Restaurant** in industry setup. The workspace retains the shared core and employee accounts. Menu, ingredients, prep batches and yields, suppliers, purchasing, floor/kitchen, reservation hours, physical counts and online ordering appear under **Operations**; restaurant books, guest credits/refunds, inventory count review, daily close and sales patterns appear under **Money**; **Staff scheduling & shifts** appears under **Team**. Branding can hide individual tools or whole groups. Earlier `/m/...` reference records remain accessible with a link to the connected workflow; they are not automatically converted into valued stock or posted accounting records.

See [prep batches and measured yields](RESTAURANT-PREP.md) for raw ingredients → work in preparation → usable prepared stock → costed menu portions.

## Set up a service day

1. Open **Operations → Restaurant orders → Settings**. Review the name, IANA timezone, business-day cutoff and tax rate. This implementation uses USD and one tax-exclusive rate, applied to the check subtotal. It does not determine the restaurant's tax obligations. Timezone and cutoff become fixed after captured reservations, physical count sheets, restaurant orders, published specials or journal activity.
2. Add suppliers and ingredients. Stock uses integer base units: grams, milliliters or each. Set reorder and target quantities. Units cannot be changed after receipts or recipes reference an ingredient.
3. Create a purchase order and mark it ordered after placing it with the supplier. Receive actual quantities, total landed costs, invoice reference and optional expiry dates. Partial receipts are supported. A receipt creates valued lots and a supplier payable in one transaction. Marking a purchase ordered does not email the supplier.
4. Add menu items with prices, stations, descriptions, kitchen-reviewed allergen information and ingredient quantities per portion. Estimated food cost remains unknown when stock costs are missing. Base portions use unexpired stock and active ingredients; selected menu options determine the final order recipe.
5. Configure tables on the **Restaurant** floor. Publish guest booking rules under **Operations → Restaurant booking hours**. Hosts can add staff reservations or walk-ins, record arrivals and quoted waits, edit upcoming visits and assign tables. Seat parties only at an available table. Open a linked dine-in check or a takeaway check, review it, then send it to the kitchen.
6. Kitchen preparation allocates stock from the earliest-expiring usable lots and posts inventory to food in preparation. Insufficient stock rolls back the complete order action. Staff advance items through cooking and ready. Mark the priced order served from Restaurant orders to post the sale, tax and food cost together.
7. Record cash actually received or an externally verified card-terminal receipt. Split tenders and additional tips are supported. These actions record evidence; they do not charge a card. Close a served, fully paid check before completing its table visit.
8. In **Money → Daily close**, record supplier payments, cash transfers, staff tip payouts and processor settlements with their fees and evidence. Finish or cancel open physical inventory counts, then count drawer cash and close the reviewed service day. The close retains cash variance, sales, tax, tips, food cost, checks and recorded covers. Mark a non-operating day closed explicitly. A close blocks additional restaurant postings into that or earlier periods.

Ingredient expiry uses the actual calendar date in the restaurant timezone, independently of the accounting-day cutoff. Unstarted, unpaid cancellations return allocated ingredients to their lots. Once any cooking has started, cancelling an unpaid, unserved order records its entire captured food cost as waste. Guest credits and recorded refunds are handled in Money as described below. Partial kitchen voids and supplier credit/return adjustments remain separate work.

## Physical inventory counts

Open **Operations → Physical inventory counts**. Start a uniquely named sheet with the ingredients to count and preparation notes. Count ingredients still in storage in grams, milliliters or each. Stock already allocated to kitchen checks is excluded; set it aside. Pause receiving, kitchen allocation, waste and returns for the selected ingredients until review is posted. Other ingredients can continue moving. Only one unfinished sheet can include an ingredient at a time.

The sheet captures each stored lot, its expected quantity, carrying value, original receipt cost and expiry. Enter an actual quantity for every lot, including zero, and explain each difference. The system never fills an expected amount into the physical observation. Expired and inactive lots remain on the sheet; expired stock remains unavailable for menu orders. Counting it does not replace recording disposal through Waste.

For a discovered lot, confirm that the restaurant already owns it and its purchase was recorded in the books. Record the original supplier, lot reference, base quantity, verified total cost, expiry and ownership/cost evidence. This is an inventory correction with no new supplier payable. If the delivery itself was never recorded, receive it first and start a fresh count. Correct or remove found-stock observations before submitting; their earlier values remain in history.

Submit only after physically checking every selected ingredient, including ingredients with no recorded lots. **Money → Inventory count review** lets a reviewer return the sheet with a reason or approve it with evidence. Operations access permits counting; Money edit access is required for posting or return. A user with both permissions can perform both steps, with their identity recorded for each. Finance-only users receive count evidence without recipes, guest contacts or floor records.

Posting atomically updates lot quantities and values, stock movements, the shared ledger, review history and retry receipt. Reductions use the remaining carrying value, including the exact remaining cents when a lot goes to zero. Positive differences in an existing lot use its original receipt unit cost; discovered lots use reviewed cost evidence. Gains and losses post against **Inventory Count Variance (6930)** and **Inventory (1300)**. A matching physical count is retained without manufacturing a financial adjustment.

Any movement of the selected ingredients—even allocation followed by a return to the original quantity—or crossing the business-day boundary invalidates the open sheet. It cannot overwrite newer stock. **Start fresh recount** cancels the old sheet and creates a linked sheet from current balances with blank observations. The original observations remain available. Posted sheets are immutable; later corrections require another physical count in an open period. Daily close requires unfinished sheets through that date to be posted or cancelled.

## Table reservations and the live floor

The owner configures bookable tables, seating duration, cleanup buffer, arrival intervals, minimum notice, booking horizon, party/covers limits, weekly service windows and holiday overrides. Overnight windows are supported. A dated exception replaces the normal windows starting on that date; an exception with no windows closes that date to new arrivals. Changing hours does not cancel existing reservations. Staff should review those bookings before announcing a closure.

Guests use `/api/website/reservations`: search a date/party size → choose a seating time → supply contact details and consent → review → confirm. Available times fit the seating plus cleanup before closing and use actual table capacity. Separate competing reviews do not hold a table; confirmation atomically rechecks availability. An exact retry does not duplicate a booking. Guests receive a private link to view, reschedule or cancel within the displayed cutoff. A failed reschedule preserves the original reservation. Instructions, duration and cancellation rules are captured when confirmed; a customer-reviewed reschedule captures the newly reviewed policy. No email or SMS is sent automatically.

In WordPress, select **Reserve a restaurant table** under **Business → Connection** and add `[shuug_restaurant_booking]`. Other websites can link to the backend page or embed it after the owner permits their exact HTTPS origin in booking settings. Guest pages expose available times without internal table names, other guests or staff records.

Times use the configured restaurant timezone. Both occurrences of repeated autumn seating times are shown with their offsets; nonexistent spring-forward times are excluded. The host's manual reservation editor also offers first/second occurrence selection. Staff may schedule a private event outside website hours, while shared table-conflict rules still apply. Imported legacy reservations without captured instants retain their local date/time and a 90-minute default turn; review them in the configured timezone.

The floor refreshes every 30 seconds and offers manual refresh. Walk-ins use the actual current restaurant date/time. Waiting parties appear by arrival, with elapsed and quoted wait minutes. Actual seating time drives table occupancy, while the originally promised time remains in the record. Waiting or seated guests from an earlier date remain visible on today's floor until resolved; they are not counted again as today's booked covers. Completing a visit requires its priced check to be paid and closed. Edits, guest reschedules, policy changes and floor actions retain revision/audit evidence.

## Online pickup and WordPress

Open **Operations → Online food ordering**. The owner enables ordering and supplies pickup instructions/contact details. Staff publish pickup times with maximum order counts. Customers currently see slots 15 minutes to two hours ahead on the current local calendar date, with a maximum of 20 portions per order.

Link your website to `/api/website/restaurant`. To embed it, allow the exact HTTPS website origin in the ordering settings. In WordPress, reload **Business → Connection**, select **Order food for pickup**, and add `[shuug_restaurant_ordering]`. The thin plugin opens the backend-hosted menu; it holds no second inventory or order database.

The customer can add differently configured items to the hosted cart, then reviews server-calculated prices, tax, dietary information and pickup time before placing an order. Submission rechecks current terms, stock and capacity and commits the priced check, stock allocation, kitchen ticket and retry receipt together. The kitchen ticket cannot begin cooking until authorized staff review its dietary notes. The private receipt link shows preparation/pickup status. Customers may cancel before preparation begins; cancellation releases stock and slot capacity. Disabling new orders preserves existing receipt links and permitted cancellations.

Online orders use **payment at pickup**. Delivery, advance-date catering, online card checkout, stacked or fixed-price promotions and automatic SMS/email notifications are still separate work. No provider messages or card charges were exercised in the acceptance tests.

## Limited specials and advertising evidence

Enable **Marketing** in the Restaurant setup questions or capability settings. **Marketing → Restaurant specials** has a normal individual hide/show toggle in Branding. Campaign editing requires Marketing edit access; publication requires the owner; recording advertising payments requires Money edit access. Finance-only users can record those payments under **Money → Restaurant books** without receiving guest or recipe records.

Create a draft with a unique code, guest description, first/last service dates, weekdays, time window, eligible channels and menu items, percentage discount, minimum food contribution per promoted portion, total/per-service-date order limits, discount budget and planned advertising budget. Overnight windows belong to the calendar date they start on. Publication validates active menu items, stock cost evidence and estimated discounted contribution. Published terms are retained; use a new code for a different offer. A paused offer may be reviewed and resumed under its original terms.

Staff enter the code while opening a check. Guests enter it on the pickup form or follow `/api/website/restaurant?special=YOURCODE`. In WordPress, use `[shuug_restaurant_ordering special="YOURCODE"]` after enabling restaurant ordering. The owner screen supplies the path and shortcode. The plugin validates the code and passes it to the backend; business rules stay in Shuug.

The shared pricing function applies one selected percentage offer to its eligible lines, rounds the line discount to cents and calculates the reviewed tax rate on the discounted subtotal. The review and receipt show the code, menu value, discount and net total. Each check captures menu revisions and offer terms. Special reviews expire after 15 minutes. An offer must still be eligible at acceptance; kitchen acceptance atomically rechecks order/discount limits, allocates stock and checks actual FEFO ingredient cost against the promoted portions' contribution minimum. Failure rolls back the entire acceptance. Concurrent requests cannot exceed the shared limits. A review alone does not reserve discount allowance or food.

Accepted orders retain their prices if the offer is paused, archived or ends. Cancelling an unpaid, unserved order releases its discount allowance. Prepared cancellations retain food waste in campaign results. The result screen reports served/pending/cancelled checks, menu value, discounts, recorded credits, net food sales, actual food cost, cancelled food waste, recorded advertising spend, and sales by ordering channel. These figures include the entire check that redeemed the code. Contribution excludes labor, overhead and payment fees; it is not incremental profit or a causal return on advertising.

Record an advertising payment only after verifying its invoice/bank evidence and that it was not already entered elsewhere. It posts Marketing & Advertising expense against Bank in the shared ledger, with duplicate-reference and closed-period checks. Spending beyond the planned budget requires an explanation and is flagged in the results. Actual paid expenses are recorded even when the plan was exceeded. Reusing the exact request after an interrupted response cannot post twice.

Publishing makes the offer redeemable in Shuug ordering. It does not send a message, buy an advertisement or publish to an external advertising account. Provider campaign publication, tracked impressions/clicks, attribution and experiments against comparable service periods remain separate work. The service-day close stops new online orders until a new open business day, while preserving configured WordPress capabilities and private existing receipts.

## Guest credits, cancellations and refunds

Open **Money → Guest credits & refunds**. The same workspace has an individual Branding visibility toggle. Reading needs Money view access; issuing a credit, cancelling a paid check or recording a refund needs Money edit access. The finance projection supplies check references, line prices, payment evidence and credit/refund history without guest contacts, recipes or kitchen notes.

For served or closed checks, enter the food amount to credit on each original line, after discounts and before tax. Amounts cannot exceed that line's remaining uncredited sale. Shuug prorates the tax captured on the original check; cumulative rounding makes a complete credit clear its exact original tax cents, even if the menu or configured tax rate later changes. Review the preview, enter a unique reference and reason, and explicitly confirm the sale and tax treatment. The original check, receipt, sale date and journal entries remain unchanged.

A credit reduces an unpaid check balance first. Any excess becomes **Customer Refunds Payable (2150)**. Food credits debit **Sales Returns and Allowances (4050)**, tax credits reduce the captured tax liability, and receivable reductions reduce **Accounts Receivable (1200)**. A partially paid guest can receive the correct credit without being charged for the disputed balance first. A served check can close once its net amount due is settled by payments or credits. Refund liabilities can remain pending across daily close and remain visible until paid.

Held tips may also be credited explicitly, up to the amount received on that check and still held in **Tips Payable (2400)**. Crediting them moves that amount to the guest refund liability so it cannot also be paid to staff. Tips already paid out require a reviewed staff-payout/payroll correction; this workflow does not invent a recovery from employees or silently classify it as a restaurant expense.

For an unserved check with a deposit, use **Cancel this unserved paid check**. Review it with the kitchen and choose any held tips to include. The whole check is cancelled and its received deposit moves from **Customer Deposits (2100)** to refunds payable. Untouched ingredients return to storage; once cooking starts, the captured food cost moves from preparation to waste. No food sale or sales-tax reversal is created for food never served. The kitchen ticket is archived and pickup capacity is released. Cancelling the record alone does not send money back.

After returning money through the original cash or card payment method, select the original receipt and record the actual refunded amount, unique external reference and payment evidence. Split refunds can be recorded against several original receipts. Each payout is capped by both the remaining guest refund liability and the unrefunded amount of its original receipt. Exact retries cannot pay the liability down twice. Cash refunds require recorded drawer funds. Card refunds reduce processor clearing; a refund after the original card settlement may leave clearing negative. When the processor actually debits the bank, record **Processor settlement → Processor debits the bank for refunds**, including reviewed fees. Provider calls and card refunds are not initiated by these recording actions.

Credits and refunds post into the current open business day. A prior closed day's evidence is preserved. Daily close separately records before-credit sales, credits and net sales, credited tax/tips, cash/card refunds and the remaining refund liability. A non-service day can still record a refund or prior-sale credit without becoming a zero-customer service observation. Weekday demand comparisons retain sales from the original service day before credits; menu/hour and special results show sales after recorded credits. Served quantities and food cost remain recorded because crediting prepared food does not restore usable ingredients.

A guest's private pickup receipt shows credits, refunds recorded, amount still due and refund still owed, without internal reasons, staff identities or processor references. This also applies when the receipt is reached through the WordPress ordering capability. Credits and payouts are immutable; later credit reversals, paid-out tip recovery and provider-initiated chargebacks still need dedicated reviewed workflows.

## Employees and scheduling

Create employee logins in **Setup → Employee accounts**. In **Team → Staff scheduling & shifts**, add assigned shifts or unassigned coverage positions. The manager reviews and publishes drafts. Assigned employees see published shifts in **My work**, acknowledge them, clock in from 30 minutes before the shift until its scheduled end, and clock out with actual unpaid break minutes. Employees can request time away; overlapping assignments must be resolved before the manager approves the request.

Editing an unattended published shift returns it to draft. Stale edits and overlapping assignments are rejected. Attendance corrections require a reason and retain the original values in audit history. Employees cannot alter another person's time or access manager records. Managers can compare planned and recorded completed hours and captured base labor rates; unknown rates and unfinished attendance remain unknown. Estimates exclude overtime, premiums, payroll taxes and benefits and do not post payroll.

Forms use the restaurant timezone (UTC before restaurant configuration), independent of device timezone. Spring daylight-saving gaps are rejected. For repeated autumn times, new wall-clock inputs use the first occurrence; review the displayed offset before publishing. The command API accepts explicit offsets when the second occurrence is required.

The shared time clock imports `timeclock.json` once into `workspace.sqlite`. A malformed original fails closed and remains unchanged. Back up the complete data directory before upgrading; retain the database when restoring so old files are not imported again.

## Owner reporting and MCP

Sales patterns now support date ranges, the preceding equal-length period, reviewed opening-time normalization, per-day comparisons, channel totals and a private service-day CSV. Record actual opening duration at close or append a reviewed correction later; original financial closes and journal entries remain unchanged. Weekday rankings require three observed open dates in each of two weekdays and complete opening-time evidence when using hourly rates. Zero-sales days count, closed days are separate, missing dates remain unknown and ties are explicit. The within-clock-hour panel still shows unnormalized serving-time totals. [Report setup, definitions and limits](RESTAURANT-REPORTS.md) explain credit posting dates, partial data and current-cost special candidates. Use Marketing → Restaurant specials to review a limited offer; reports do not buy advertisements or establish causal results.

Restaurant journal entries feed the shared general ledger. Recipe stock movements, kitchen work, tenders, payables, journal entries and command retry receipts commit atomically. The MCP exposes `restaurant_report`, `restaurant_catalog`/`restaurant_command`, `dining_catalog`/`dining_command` and `schedule_catalog`/`schedule_command`; discover input contracts before writing, and reuse the exact request ID for an interrupted action. Opening-time review, menu option definitions, selected check-line choices, specific-line credit/cancellation/refund recording, stocktake preparation/review, special lifecycle and verified-spend commands are included in `restaurant_catalog`. Read `restaurant`, `restaurant-accounting`, `restaurant-dining`, `restaurant-specials`, `restaurant-stocktakes`, `restaurant-credits` and `staff-schedule` through `business_read`. MCP backend credentials authorize the entire client workspace; personal schedule actions identify that backend actor as the owner rather than impersonating employees.

See [the acceptance audit](RESTAURANT-ACCEPTANCE.md) for remaining restaurant requirements and verification evidence. This is not yet a claim of complete restaurant or enterprise readiness.
