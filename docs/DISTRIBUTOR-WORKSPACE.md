# U.S. hot sauce distribution workspace

Goal: make Shuug feel like an accounting-led operating system for a U.S. hot sauce distributor, with the ease of a familiar small-business bookkeeping app.

The default experience must answer: what did we sell, what do customers owe, what do we owe, which cases need to leave, what stock needs attention, and what should we do next? Custom dashboards and growth tools remain available behind the core daily workflow.

Completion requirements and evidence to gather:

- A clear distributor home with a period selector, sales and gross margin, customer invoice balances, expenses, orders awaiting fulfillment, product stock and direct next actions. Values need visible source/period/unknown-state handling.
- A useful Money workspace with real accounting invoice/bill balances when QuickBooks is connected, clear stale/disconnected states, and quick access to reviewed expenses and business history. No order totals or campaign budgets presented as paid cash or actual ad spend.
- A reliable case/bottle workflow: exact case equivalents, combined duplicate SKU lines, no repeated shipment deductions, no cancelled-order shipping, no fabricated purchased labels or carrier confirmations, and durable stock/lot/shipment records.
- U.S. defaults and domain language: USD, U.S. business dates, bottles/cases, wholesale payment terms, store/restaurant/distributor accounts, territory visits, buyer POs, product costs and lot/expiry history. Imported data must remain intact.
- Stable, easy navigation and simple daily actions. Advanced dashboard editing, marketing and automation must not replace the daily selling, bookkeeping and delivery flow.
- Meaningful calculation/persistence/authorization tests, a production build, HTTP workflow checks, and inspection at the UI surface through the configured native testing tools. Native tool failures must remain explicit verification gaps.

This document is the completion checklist, not a declaration that these requirements are already satisfied. Implementation and verification results will be recorded as the work proceeds.
