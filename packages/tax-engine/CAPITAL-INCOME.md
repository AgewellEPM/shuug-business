# Qualified dividends and ordinary investment securities

The 2023–2025 implementation connects encrypted source input, browser editing, Schedule B, Schedule D, Form 8949, the Form 1040 Qualified Dividends and Capital Gain Tax Worksheet, the QBI taxable-income limitation, limited Form 8960, and next-year capital-loss workpapers. This is an engineering implementation with bounded scope. It is not an agency certification, approved filing service, tax-professional sign-off, or a complete individual return product. Exported packets remain marked draft.

## Source records and supported scope

`individual.capitalIncome` is optional. Its `trades` contain a stable ID, security/quantity description, Form 8949 category A–F, acquisition and sale dates, original integer-cent proceeds/basis, documented adjustments, and a source reference. Sale dates must fall in the return year. Ordinary acquisition dates verify the short/long category; `Various` requires explicit lot holding-period review. Covered/noncovered and no-1099 securities remain distinct. Every trade is reported on Form 8949, including covered trades eligible for direct Schedule D reporting, to retain the lot record in the packet.

Supported adjustments are B (incorrect 1099-B basis), E (ordinary selling costs omitted from basis/proceeds), T (wrong broker holding-period classification), and W (wash-sale loss). Covered basis corrections preserve reported basis and use a signed adjustment. Noncovered 1099-B corrections enter correct basis directly, code B and zero adjustment. T has zero adjustment and uses the corrected category. E must be nonpositive; W must be nonnegative and cannot exceed the loss after other adjustments. Each adjustment needs an explanation, and review must establish wash-sale matching and replacement basis across accounts, spouses and retirement accounts. The software does not discover or infer replacement lots.

Capital-gain distributions represent ordinary Form 1099-DIV box 2a amounts only. Separate positive short/long prior loss inputs retain character. They require a reviewed prior-year worksheet, including ownership, filing-status changes and debt/basis modifications. Qualified dividends remain an eligible subset of the ordinary-dividend payer amount in `investmentIncome`; they are never added to ordinary dividends a second time.

The supported scope excludes collectibles, unrecaptured section 1250 gains, sections 1202/1244, business/property/home sales, options/short sales, market discount, digital assets, qualified opportunity funds, installment and like-kind transactions, gifts/inheritances, pass-through capital items, Form 4952 elections and other unsupported adjustments. The scope, basis, holding period, adjustments and carryovers require explicit review. Failure leaves trusted gain, AGI, tax, QBI and balance outputs unavailable. The NIIT review separately establishes that these gains/losses/distributions and carryovers have identical regular-tax and NIIT treatment. Complex items cannot be entered as unexplained adjustment overrides.

## Calculation and rendering

Each Form 8949 proceeds, basis and total-adjustment column is rounded to whole dollars; its gain/loss is calculated from those displayed columns. Category totals sum the displayed rows, preserving exact cross-form arithmetic. Original cents and adjustment explanations remain in the encrypted record and PDF supporting statement. The official 2023/2024 forms hold 14 trades per part; the changed 2025 form holds 11. Overflow creates additional category-specific copies, with no discarded records. Unused parts of each two-page official template are blank apart from identity. Source descriptions wrap within the official cell; unsupported glyphs or text/amount overflow fail export instead of truncating.

Schedule D nets short and long results with their respective prior carryovers. Positive net capital gain eligible for preferential rates is the positive lesser of its long-term and total net gain. A net loss reduces Form 1040 by at most $3,000 ($1,500 married filing separately). The next-year carryforward worksheet uses signed taxable income before the Form 1040 zero floor so a deduction unused because income is below deductions remains a carryover. Short losses are used first. The next-year amounts are recorded, not automatically imported into another return; future-year ownership/status changes need review.

All 25 lines of the annual qualified-dividend worksheet are retained. The ordinary portion uses the actual annual Tax Table below $100,000 and annual Tax Computation Worksheet above it. Preferential income uses the annual 0%/15%/20% thresholds, and total tax is limited to the all-ordinary tax alternative. No special-rate Schedule D Tax Worksheet treatment is inferred. The same positive net capital gain plus qualified dividends reduces Form 8995's taxable-income limit.

Form 8960 line 5a uses the capital amount actually included in Form 1040, including the capped loss; its line 12 floors total net investment income at zero. Ordinary dividends include the qualified subset exactly once. Any NIIT difference, excluded gain, carryover adjustment, additional loss, MAGI change or Part II deduction requires additional implementation/review. See [INVESTMENT-INCOME.md](INVESTMENT-INCOME.md).

An absent capital record remains compatible with older returns and the provisional 2026 ordinary-income path. Selecting capital or detailed investment records for 2026 creates a blocking annual-pack diagnostic, unknown totals and no borrowed 2025 capital forms.

Annual official PDFs and field geometry are pinned in each `forms/<year>` pack. `test/capital-reference.json` pins actual downloaded bytes/SHA-256 for twelve annual instruction/publication PDFs. Its four Form 8949 column-h examples and Bob/Shelly capital-loss example come from the annual IRS publications. Example 3's use of supported code E is a synthetic interpretation of the published generic arithmetic. Preferential-tax and carryforward-character vectors are separately labeled synthetic arithmetic, not IRS taxpayer examples.

`test/capital-income.test.mjs` checks those vectors, all filing-status threshold edges, source validation, signed adjustments, review gates, legacy compatibility, QBI and NIIT integration. `test/capital-pdf.test.mjs` extracts actual flattened annual PDF output with the bounded temporary-file `pdf-text.mjs` helper and checks printed row coordinates, identities/categories, totals, overflow copies, loss workpapers and blank unknown amounts. `packages/secure-tax-client/test/capital-editor.test.mjs` exercises the real editor module using synthetic DOM events, signed amount input, encrypted-save callback input, reopening, year conflicts and private-value clearing. This is programmatic integration evidence, not desktop visual acceptance or proof of agency acceptance.
