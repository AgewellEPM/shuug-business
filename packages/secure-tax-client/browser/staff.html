Recover after device loss
  1. Import your encrypted device-key backup with its separate passphrase. This restores the same cryptographic device identity; it does not bypass a withdrawn or revoked grant.
  2. If the key backup is lost, create a new staff device key and save its public request. Ask the firm to verify the new fingerprint and withdraw the lost device’s grants.
  3. In the separately trusted firm recovery environment, the operator checks the signed client enrollment and exact source ciphertext, then signs fresh read grants and per-object capsules for the replacement key. Only those public artifacts are published here.
  4. Sign in with MFA, unlock the replacement key, refresh signed access and verify the recovered records before retiring any remaining backup.
No firm recovery or signing private key is generated or imported in this browser. A password or authenticator reset alone cannot recover encrypted data. Existing plaintext, old backups and trusted delivery/custody still need their own migration and operational review.
