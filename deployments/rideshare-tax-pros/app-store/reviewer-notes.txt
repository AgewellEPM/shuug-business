Gig Tax Pros opens to local tools without sign-in. No username or password is needed to review the local journal, receipt entry, offer screenshots, sample shift and getting-started guide. Dismiss the introductory guide to reach Home. Firm account features require an invitation and a configured workspace; the hosted workspace is currently unavailable and no working review account is provided. Bank and provider account connections cannot be reviewed in this state. There is no native self-registration form.

LOCAL JOURNAL
Open Home. Use Trips > Add manual trip to record mileage, or Expense to enter a cost and choose/scan a receipt. Review recognized merchant and amount before saving. The app also supports income entries, classification and journal export.

OFFER REVIEW
Open Offers > Choose screenshot, select a gig platform, then choose an offer image. Recognition runs on-device. Review the extracted pay, total distance, time and estimated costs before saving; missing values remain unknown. The user makes acceptance decisions in the original provider app. The app does not read another app's private notification inbox.

OPTIONAL SCREEN SHARING
ReplayKit offer reading requires a physical device and an explicit user-started sharing session. In Account > Read gig offers, enable a reader shift and use Apple's screen-sharing control. Users can stop sharing or disable the reader. The app does not retain full broadcast frames/video or unrelated transcripts. Parsed offer observations remain local.

MILEAGE AND PERMISSIONS
Background location supports a user-started drive and optional driving/stop assistance. Location and motion permissions are requested for those features and assistance can be disabled in Account. End a drive to stop its recording. Raw route coordinates remain on-device. Requested Apple Maps lookups send endpoints/searches to Apple. GPS gaps and revoked permissions can leave incomplete records.

SAMPLE CONTENT AND PROVIDERS
Home > Explore charts & offers with sample data opens a clearly labeled built-in example; it does not change the user's journal. Bank and gig connectors depend on the firm's authorized provider setup and remain unavailable when unconfigured. There are no native paid digital subscriptions or in-app purchases. The app does not file tax returns or guarantee earnings or tax outcomes.
